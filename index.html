How to play

  • Build each suit up from Ace to King on the four foundations (top right).
  • In the columns, place a card on one rank higher of the opposite color (red 6 on black 7).
  • The four free cells (top left) each hold one card. Any card can go in an empty column.
  • You can move a run of cards at once if there are enough free cells and empty columns to make the move one card at a time.
  • Tap a card to move it to the best spot. Drag to choose where it goes.
  • Cards that are no longer needed go to the foundations automatically.
